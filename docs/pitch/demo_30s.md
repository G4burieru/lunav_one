Challenge submission. No slide presentation is planned. It is separate from the [Extended Demo](demo_script.md), which is complementary
material. Aim for a **29–29.5 second final export**, including titles and the
ending, rather than using the entire limit.

## Complete narration

> Team Lunav One. Moon explorers need sunlight and communication. Lunav One
> explores how terrain changes sunlight and Earth visibility. Choose a location,
> move through time, and watch its Sun and Earth indicators. NASA’s open data
> powers the model. An educational tool for understanding lunar conditions, not
> certifying safe landings. Explore the Moon. Understand the limits.

The narration has **55 words**, approximately **22–24 seconds at 140–150 words
per minute**, leaving about 5–7 seconds for pauses and screen transitions. This is an estimate, not a measured recording. Rehearse with the
actual voice and adjust pauses to the table; leave the final seconds for the
closing message. Do not speed up the voice to rescue a take that runs long.

## Recording and timing checks

- Preload the app, NASA imagery and Connecting Ridge C1. Keep a covered 2027–2028
  scenario at 2 m; rehearse real instants with a visible badge change.
- Use actual app recordings. Cuts may remove waits but must not invent a state
  transition or imply that a Site Card average changes with the selected hour.
- Solar fill represents the visible fraction of the Sun's disk; blue Earth means
  geometric Earth Visibility, gray/crossed Earth means the criterion is not met.
  These are not electrical power or a guaranteed radio link.
- The short take omits the Site Planner, neighborhood calculation and scientific
  comparison details; those belong in the Extended Demo. Do not attempt all 17
  sites in this video.
- Keep captions short and synchronized. Use direct cuts or very brief dissolves
  inside the allocated intervals, without adding transition time.
- Check the complete exported file with a media player's duration display. Keep
  it below 30 seconds; include every title, fade, silence and end frame in that
  measurement.
